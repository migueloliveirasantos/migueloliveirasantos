I'm a Brazilian student exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer science, and cognitive neuroscience.

My interests lie in understanding how people learn, how technology shapes cognition, and how computational systems can help us solve meaningful problems.

I'm currently building Roasted AI, an AI-powered learning platform grounded in the Socratic method and Mediated Learning Experience (MLE) theory. Rather than providing immediate answers, Roasted guides students through questions and feedback to encourage independent thinking and deeper understanding.
